Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains how the independent creative-media project Love a Little, Feel a Little More, operated through the Pabrik Konten Otomatis project, handles information in connection with this website and its content-production workflows.

1. This website

This website is informational. We do not operate visitor accounts, contact forms, or our own advertising or analytics trackers on this site. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to include in that message and use it to respond.

The site may be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical information, such as server logs and connection data, under its own policies and terms.

2. Google Drive integration

If enabled by the project owner, production workflows may connect to Google Drive through Google OAuth. The integration is intended to create and manage project files, such as rendered videos, audio, quality-check reports, manifests, and related folders. It is designed to request the limited drive.file permission rather than general access to the user's entire Drive.

Authorization is handled by Google. The project does not ask for or store the user's Google account password. The owner can review or revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings. Google handles account authentication and related data under Google's own privacy terms.

3. GitHub and automated workflows

Project code and automation may run through GitHub repositories and GitHub Actions. Access tokens and OAuth credentials should be stored as encrypted repository secrets, not committed to source code. Workflow logs may contain operational information such as job status, filenames, timestamps, and upload outcomes. Repository content and workflow records are subject to GitHub's policies, settings, and retention practices.

4. How project information is used

Information processed by the workflows is used to operate content production, store and organize project assets, deliver notifications, troubleshoot failures, and protect the project. We do not sell personal information.

5. Storage and retention

Production files stored in the project owner's Google Drive remain there until the owner deletes them or changes the relevant storage settings. GitHub content and workflow records are retained according to repository configuration and GitHub's applicable policies. Email correspondence may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond and maintain records.

6. Third-party services

The project may rely on third-party services such as Google, GitHub, and providers of media or source materials. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices. We encourage users to review the policies of any service they authorize or use.

7. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards, including limited permissions and protected secrets, to reduce unauthorized access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Project credentials should never be shared publicly or committed to a repository.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website or production workflows change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact gadingsmart515@gmail.com.
